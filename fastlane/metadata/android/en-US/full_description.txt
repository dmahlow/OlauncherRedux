OlauncherRedux is a minimal, text-only Android launcher. It is a fork of OlauncherCF (archived), which was a fork of Olauncher.

What is new in OlauncherRedux:

- App groups on the home screen. Put several apps behind one name and open them with a tap.
- Sort the app drawer by most used. Apps you open often move to the top. Needs the "Usage access" permission.
- Optional small app icons in the drawer.
- Tap an empty home screen slot to pick an app.
- A bundled dark wallpaper that fits the text-only look.
- Better defaults: 10 slots, larger text, apps on the right and bottom, dark theme.

Features from OlauncherCF:

- Text-only home screen with up to 15 shortcuts
- Gestures for swipe up, down, left, right, clock, date and double tap
- Rename and hide apps
- Independent clock and date position
- Text size and alignment settings
- Backup and restore
- No ads, no links, no internet permission

OlauncherRedux uses the following permissions:

- android.permission.SET_WALLPAPER: to set the bundled wallpaper when you ask for it.
- android.permission.EXPAND_STATUS_BAR: to open the notification shade with a gesture.
- android.permission.QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES: to list installed apps in the drawer.
- com.android.alarm.permission.SET_ALARM: to open the alarm app when you tap the clock.
- android.permission.REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES: to show the uninstall dialog for an app.
- android.permission.PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS: optional, for sorting by most used.
